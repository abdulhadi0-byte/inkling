The hand-signs

  • ☝️
    One finger up
    Draw — your index fingertip becomes the nib.
  • ✌️
    Two fingers
    Hover — move the cursor without laying ink.
  • 🤟
    Three fingers
    Cycle the ink colour.
  • 🖐️
    Open palm
    Eraser — wipe the canvas near your hand.
  • ✊
    Fist, held a beat
    Clear the whole page.

Works best in a well-lit room, hand roughly 30–70cm from the camera.

Try the kaleidoscope toggle for a mirrored mandala effect, or dissolve for ink that slowly fades like it's in water.
